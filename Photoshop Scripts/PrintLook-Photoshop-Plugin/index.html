Each colour layer of your artwork is one printing plate with one ink. Render prints them, bottom layer first, onto a new layer at the top; your artwork is untouched. Preview prints just the marquee selection. Choosing a look sets everything below it; the tick boxes on the section headers switch whole stages on and off. Settings marked est. have defaults that are estimates, not documented figures.
