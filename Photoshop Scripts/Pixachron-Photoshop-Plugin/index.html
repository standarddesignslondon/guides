which direction is time?

▾Chain

Stages run in series: the picture comes out of one and goes into the next. Each stage has its own path, effect and signal settings below; the mask and output are shared. The selected stage is the one the Path, Effect and Signal sections edit.

▾Path (the direction of time)

Rolling back along a different path scrambles the picture by the two paths alone, before any effect.

▾Mask

Layer transparency needs Source set to the selected layer. "Only masked pixels" skips the rest entirely: they are neither changed nor counted as time.

▾Effect

▾Signal

AC takes the slow-moving level out along the path before the effect and puts it back after, so feedback builds on edges and detail rather than on the picture's overall tone (which otherwise runs away to white or black). DC is the raw databending behaviour.

▾Outline mode

Direction runs the effect over the angle the outline is heading in, then walks it again: low-pass rounds corners, bitcrush makes a pixel font, delay echoes every corner. Position runs it over x and y as two channels: the shape itself echoes, smears and shuffles.
In outline mode the Path and Mask sections don't apply (only Time runs backwards does); each stage's effect, signal and mix do, with ⏱ distances measured in pixels along the outline. Preview and Render both make a new path; Update replaces the last one.

▾Output

Preview renders the whole picture at the preview size, with all pixel distances scaled to match, and writes it (upsampled) to a "Pixachron preview" layer. Render does it at full size. Update re-renders the last result in place.
