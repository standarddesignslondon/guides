Cheap-print look: optionally halftones the image, then knocks its colour plates out of register and roughs up the surface. Anything at 0 is skipped.

Print

0 = no halftone. 4–10 is a newspaper feel at print resolution.
Each colour plate drifts by its own random amount and direction, up to this.

Paper

A warm photo-filter wash, like aged stock or cheap ink on off-white.
