03

kurzwellen

Shortwave-radio simulator — makes anything sound like it is being received rather than played.

03.0

about

Not another tape device. Tape degradation is mechanical; shortwave degradation is atmospheric, and the signatures are completely different — multipath comb filtering that sweeps as the ionosphere moves, fades that an AGC chases back up, a receiver hissing into its own noise floor, and interference arriving in bursts. Nine modules, each with a toggle and an amount, all breathing together under one shared drift source: the coherence of everything degrading at the same time is what sells it. Named for Stockhausen's Kurzwellen (1968), for players with shortwave receivers.

03.1

quick start

  1. 01Drop Kurzwellen.amxd on an audio track. Play something with a voice in it.
  2. 02That's most of it — the defaults are the classic "voice on a distant station".
  3. 03Bandwidth is how much radio. Fade is how much ionosphere.
  4. 04Shape and Depth set how the whole device moves. Depth 0 freezes everything and costs nothing in level.
03.2

controls

receiver

The chain models propagation then reception: Width → Tune → Fade → Band → Grit → AGC → Events → +Het → Mix.

Bandwidth1.0 – 8.0 kHz
The IF filter, and the thing that makes it a radio. Fixed 90 Hz high-pass plus a 4-pole low-pass, 24 dB/oct — shortwave sounds band-limited, not merely darkened. Always on; there is no bypass.
Width0 – 100
Stereo → mono collapse. 100 is bit-exact passthrough, 0 is full L+R mono. Defaults to 0 because real shortwave is mono.
Mix0 – 100
Dry/wet. 100 mutes the dry entirely — this is usually an insert "make it radio" effect, so that is the default.

ionosphere

Fade0 – 100 + toggle
Multipath. Direct plus two delayed copies (0.7 and 1.9 ms) whose times and gains drift independently, making a slowly sweeping comb — the hollow, almost-reverby character. Plus a flat fade of up to −14 dB on the slowest drift phase. Sets blend only; Depth sets how far things move.
Tune−150 … +150 Hz + toggle
A frequency shifter, not a pitch shifter: every partial moves by a fixed number of Hz, so the harmonic series goes inharmonic. That is the queasy metallic sound of a mistuned SSB receiver. Centre-detented — ±2 Hz snaps to zero, and at zero it is exactly zero.

detector and gain

Grit0 – 100 + toggle
Detector distortion plus signal-correlated noise. The noise is multiplied by the input envelope, so it makes the signal gritty and dies when the input stops — it will not fight anything layered underneath. Subtle around 20–35, proper AM overmodulation crunch at 100.
AGC0 – 100 + toggle
The receiver's gain stage. Boost only, up to +18 dB, slewing down fast and up slowly. That recovery lag is the breathing, and it is what chases Fade's flat fades back up — blooming slightly loud before it settles.

interference

Events0 – 100 + toggle
Density, from roughly one every 20 s at 10 to one every 1.5 s at 100. Three types, weighted 4/2/2: Dropout (ducks 20–40 dB for 60–400 ms, and dips the AGC detector too, so you get a swell afterwards), Crash (band-passed noise burst, 80–300 ms decay, louder at higher density) and Flutter (200–600 ms of 8–20 Hz shallow AM, 6 dB deep).
Hetero0 – 100 + toggle
The adjacent-carrier whistle. One sine wandering 400–2800 Hz with a slow amplitude wobble so it never sounds like a test tone. Defaults off.

conditions

One generator with eight decorrelated output phases, one per consumer — not eight LFOs. Everything degrades together, which is the whole illusion.

ShapeSine / Drift / Jump / Iono
Sine plain LFO, predictable. Drift smoothed random walk — the default, and the one that sounds most like real propagation. Jump sample-and-hold steps. Iono Drift with sudden lurches every 10–30 s, which also double the event rate for about a second afterwards.
Rate0.02 – 2 Hz
Log-scaled. The flat fade deliberately rides the slowest phase, so at the default 0.12 Hz it takes around 25 seconds to come round.
Depth0 – 100
Scales every consumer's modulation span. 0 freezes all drift — still filtered and gritty, but stationary — and costs exactly nothing in level.

03.4

workflow

it processes only what you feed it

There is no background static bed — that was left out on purpose. Layer real atmosphere from the SW Radio device underneath if you want it. What Kurzwellen does generate is receiver behaviour: correlated detector noise, AGC hiss, static crashes and the heterodyne whistle.

set the weather first, then the radio

Shape, Rate and Depth decide how the whole device moves; the module amounts decide how much of each symptom you hear. Getting the Conditions right first makes the rest quick.

iono is the one to reach for when it feels too even

Lurches arrive every 10–30 s and drag everything with them, and they double the event rate while they last. Bad conditions cluster; that is what stops long passages sounding like an LFO.

the whistle re-tunes itself in jump and iono

It rides the same Conditions phase everything else does, so when that phase steps, the whistle steps with it — like a neighbouring station being retuned. No separate control for it.

03.5

gotchas

  • AGC only boosts. On a signal already above its target it correctly sits at unity and does nothing — that is what an AGC does, not a fault. It earns its keep on quieter material and on anything Fade is already pulling down.
  • With AGC on, silence does not go fully silent. The receiver turns itself up into its own noise floor between phrases: about −55 dB at the default 45, rising to a −30 dB ceiling at 100. Turn AGC off if that is a nuisance.
  • Width does not deepen Fade's comb, despite what you might expect. Fade processes left and right with shared control signals, so each channel gets an identically-defined comb whatever the source correlation — measured, it is 10.7 dB at Width 0 against 10.8 dB at Width 100. Width is a stereo-image utility, nothing more.
  • Tune's wander scales with the dial. At centre it is exactly 0 Hz. That is deliberate: applied at centre the wander was the entire shift, and ±6.6 Hz is 93 cents on a 120 Hz voice — nearly a semitone of wobble.
  • Every module is bit-exact transparent at amount 0 or toggle off. All eight of them, verified individually and together.
03.6

rebuilding

python3 build_kurzwellen.py regenerates the .amxd + .maxpat with full validation. No freeze is ever needed — there is no external js anywhere.

python3 sim_kurzwellen.py runs 11 suites mirroring the DSP maths (about 150 s). --full adds the 60 s stability soak.

Rebuilding from the script alone into an empty folder produces byte-identical files. Never rescue a copy from an old Live Set.
