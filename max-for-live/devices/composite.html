composite

Photoshop blend modes for two sample layers — Darken, Multiply, Difference and the rest, applied per frequency instead of per pixel.

23.0

about

Two sample players — layer A on top, layer B beneath — meet in an FFT where each frequency bin's energy is treated as a pixel's brightness, and all nine blend modes from the layers panel do what they do in Photoshop: Darken keeps only what both layers share, Multiply makes A mask B like a vocoder, Difference cancels the common material and leaves a ghost. Layers warp to Live's grid or free-run and drift; loudness self-calibrates to the material via an adaptive white point. Fully self-contained, performs from Push 3 in Session view.

23.1

quick start

  1. 01Drop Composite.amxd on an audio track and drop a sample on each slot.
  2. 02Pick a Mode — start with Darken on two different loops, or Multiply with drums on A and a pad on B.
  3. 03Opacity is the layers-panel opacity: 100% = the blend, 0% = layer B alone.
  4. 04Turn SYNC off on one layer so the loops drift — the mask evolves instead of repeating.
  5. 05Freeze a sustained layer (FRZ) and play material through it: the held spectrum becomes a fixed stencil.

23.4

workflow

the frozen stencil

Freeze a pad mid-chord, switch to Darken, and run a busy loop on the other layer — only the frequencies of the held chord pass through. Multiply does the same but shaped by the moving layer's dynamics.

drifting knockouts

Two loops of different lengths with SYNC off phase against each other, so Darken's overlap — and the mask — never repeats. Resolution down and Contrast up makes the movement bold.

difference as a lens

Two takes or two mixes of the same material in Difference: everything they share cancels and you hear only what changed.

23.5

gotchas

  • The spectral chain adds 85 ms of latency. Synced layers pre-read by exactly that and land on the grid; knob changes and free-running layers arrive 85 ms late — normal for FFT devices.
  • Bar maths assume 4/4; odd meters lock to the beat count only.
  • Multiply is the geometric mean sqrt(a·b), not the raw product — the true Photoshop product doubles the darkness in dB and buries the output. Raise Contrast for the darker character.
  • Loop Length has a floor of about 50 ms.

rebuilding

node build_composite.js in Composite Device/ regenerates the patcher and .amxd from scratch; node tests/sim_composite.js runs the 546-check suite (structural asserts plus a real-FFT port of the whole spectral chain). No external js — never needs freezing.
