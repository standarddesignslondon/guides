misregistered print

The cheap-print look: halftone dots, colour plates out of register, paper grain and warmth.

06.0

about

Treats the active layer as a badly printed reproduction of itself. Optionally halftones the image first, then splits it into colour plates and drifts each by its own random distance and direction — the blue plate anchors, red and green wander — before finishing with paper grain and a warm photo-filter wash like aged stock. Anything at 0 is skipped, so it also serves as a plain halftone or plain warmth pass.

06.1

quick start

  1. 01Select a pixel layer and press Run pass; the defaults give a modest newspaper drift.
  2. 02For heavier riso-style separation, push misregistration to 10 – 20 px and add halftone.
06.2

controls

print

Halftone dot sizepx, 0 = off
Photoshop's colour halftone at standard screen angles. 4 – 10 px reads as newsprint at print resolution; values below 4 are treated as off.
Misregistration maxpx
Each drifting plate picks its own random direction and a distance between 40% and 100% of this.

paper

Paper grain%
Monochrome noise over everything.
Warmth%
A warming photo filter, luminosity preserved — cheap ink on off-white.
06.3

workflow

order with the others

This one usually goes last: halftone dots and plate drift read as the final reproduction of whatever mess the other passes made. Static over the top afterwards breaks the dots up nicely.

06.4

gotchas

  • Halftone below 4 px is ignored — the filter needs room for a dot.
  • Misregistration fringes are colour by definition; on a mono image, desaturate after this pass and the fringes grey out to tonal wobble, which can also be the point.
06.5

rebuilding

For quick iteration, load the folder's manifest.json in UXP Developer Tools instead — dev-loads are live-reloadable but vanish when Photoshop restarts.

To update the installed copy: bump version in manifest.json, zip manifest.json + index.html + main.js (at the archive root) with a .ccx extension, and run the installer agent again.

06.6

installing

  1. 01In Terminal, run Adobe's installer agent on the plugin's .ccx from Photoshop Scripts/CCX Installers: "/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Adobe Desktop Common/RemoteComponents/UPI/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent.app/Contents/MacOS/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent" --install <plugin>.ccx
  2. 02Restart Photoshop. The panel appears under Plugins and survives restarts — UXP Developer Tools is not needed.
  3. 03Do not double-click the .ccx: Creative Cloud wrongly reports Compatible app required for unsigned plugins.
